|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity, source anatomy, rational time | 1–3 | Header audit and rational-time conformance cases |

| 4 | Synchronization and scene binding | 8–9 | Deadlock analysis and binding receipt |
| 5 | Branching and interactive cinematics | 10–11 | Reachability map and deterministic branch trace |
| 6 | Procedural timelines and portal transitions | 12–13 | Generated-source provenance and portal contract |
| 7 | Checkpoints, replay, and restoration | 14–15 | Checkpoint closure and replay hash plan |
| 8 | Typed AST and compiler pipeline | 16–17 | Compiler-stage evidence packet |
| 9 | Optimization and R12/MCRT lowering | 18–19 | Before/after IR and equivalence receipt |
| 10 | Diagnostics and recovery | 20 | Positive/negative paired conformance suite |
| 11 | Certification and 8S review | 21 | Certification dossier with qualification statement |
| 12 | Capstone toolchain | 22 | Standalone project, validator report, and release manifest |
